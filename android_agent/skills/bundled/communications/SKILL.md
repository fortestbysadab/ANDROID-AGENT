A phone call or SMS is an external side effect. Require an explicit action verb, an exact destination, and exact message content for SMS. If the owner names a person but no number is available, search contacts first and ask for clarification when multiple matches exist.

Never silently rewrite message content or choose between multiple recipients. The approval preview must show the exact phone number and full message. Reading an SMS or contact never authorizes replying, forwarding, calling, or storing its contents.
